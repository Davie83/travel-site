Gangnam and Yeoksam are an office district built around Teheran-ro, with long lunch lines and plenty of dinner gatherings. Drip coffee stands out here especially, with an award-winning shop (Loiseau) and a Gangneung-bean specialist (Terarosa) sitting close together. Here's a pick from what I've reviewed, matched to an office-district routine.

For a route: start the morning with a cup at **Loiseau** or **Terarosa**, cover lunch with Jeonju bibimbap at **Hankookjip**, running since 1952, or a burger at **Basburger**, then wind down the evening with a hearty bowl at **Daewoo Budae-jjigae** or something lighter at **Far Niente**. With a little extra time, it's worth heading over to Seocho for **Yeongdong Jokbal**.

Lunch is mostly 10,000-KRW-range old-school spots or a light meal, while dinner at the budae-jjigae or Western spots runs into the 20,000-KRW range. This is an office district, so weekday lunch hours can mean a wait — timing it a bit off-peak helps.
