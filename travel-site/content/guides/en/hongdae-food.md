Hongdae's strength is places open late. A ramen shop running over twenty years, a snow-crab course, an izakaya with a line before opening, and a makgeolli bar are spread from Hongik University Station out to Sangsu and Hapjeong. Here's a pick from what I've reviewed, matched to a route running from afternoon into late night.

For a route: take a daytime break with the cats at **Roofcatme**, right by Hongik Univ. Station Exit 9, then cover dinner with ramen at **Hakata Bunko** or **Butanchu**, or a snow-crab course at **Kim & Kim**. As the night goes on, it flows naturally into aged sashimi at **Yokosuka Tsunami** or makgeolli at **Sangsu Jutaek** toward Sangsu, and pork ribs at **Rokgalbi** toward Hapjeong.

**Hakata Bunko** runs until 3 a.m., and **Yokosuka Tsunami** draws a line before it even opens, so build in some slack. Hongik University Station, Sangsu, and Hapjeong are all walkable from each other, so a single day's route can run from an afternoon cafe stop all the way to late-night ramen.
