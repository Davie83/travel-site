Myeongdong is a shopping district that's always busy with tourists, but the side streets still hold old-school restaurants and quiet cafes. Everything here is within walking distance, so it's easy to fold into a single day. Here's a pick from what I've reviewed, in an order that's easy to slot into a sightseeing route.

For a route: start lunch with kalguksu at **Myeongdong Gyoja**, take an afternoon breather at **Tailor Coffee**, then wind down in the evening with okonomiyaki at **Tsuruhashi Fugetsu** or mild namul bibimbap at **Mokmyeoksanbang**, at the foot of Namsan.

Most of these are either 10,000-KRW-range old-school spots or light meals around 15,000 KRW, so it's easy to work one in between shopping stops without much of a dent in the budget.
