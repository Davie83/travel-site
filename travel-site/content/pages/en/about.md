## What you will find here

- [Food](food) — places I have eaten at, from Seoul to Jeju. Besides the regional lists, you can browse by kind of food: hangover soup, sashimi, gomtang and seolleongtang, set meals and more.
- [Places](travel) — beaches, markets, parks and sights I have actually been to.
- [Travel tips](tips) — the things that trip up first-time visitors, such as transit cards, changing money and getting from the airport into the city.

## What the guides cover

Not "this place was lovely". The things you would want to know the day before you go:

- What order to walk a neighbourhood in, and how long it really takes
- Roughly what it costs per person
- Whether you can park, and how public transport works
- When the crowds show up, and when they don't
- The bits that confuse first-time visitors — how to order, cash vs card, portion sizes

## What is inside one guide

- **An at-a-glance table** — address, phone, opening hours, the signature dish and its price, and the nearest subway station in one place.
- **My own photos and a description** — the table or the place as it actually looked.
- **Menu words and one phrase to order with** — a Korean sentence you can say as it is, for example "Baguette juseyo".
- **Who it suits** — who will like it and what falls short, marked with ✅ and ⚠️.

## How these guides are written

- I write only what I confirmed myself. Anything I could not verify is left out, and a dish I have not tried is said to be untried.
- Address, phone and opening hours are checked against the map and the venue's own listing before publishing.
- Anything that changes — a price, an opening time — is marked with the month it was checked, like **(as of Oct 2026)**, and what I do not know is said to be unknown.
- No rankings, no "best in Seoul" claims.
- No guide was written for payment from a venue.

## Languages

The Korean guide is the original, and the English, Japanese and Chinese versions carry the same content in those languages. Shop names come with their Hangul so you can find them on the ground, and the facts follow the Korean original. If a translation reads oddly or is wrong, please tell me.

## Who writes it

One person writes and publishes everything. There are no guest posts and no comment section.

The writer is **Davie**, a Google Maps **Local Guide (Level 6)** whose photos have passed **210,000 views** (as of Sep 2026). The posts here are the long-form version of those visits and of newer trips since. The profile is public and anyone can check it — [Google Maps contributor profile](https://www.google.com/maps/contrib/108511658203720708025)

Not every post here has a matching Google Maps review. Some visits were posted there, some were not.

You can react to a guide with an emoji at the bottom of the page. No sign-in, no personal details, and a second tap cancels it. It helps me see which guides people find useful so I can write more like them.

## Advertising and affiliate links

To help cover running costs I am preparing **Google AdSense** ads, and no ads are shown at the moment. When they appear, they will be marked apart from the guide text.

A few guides, such as the airport transport notes in the travel tips, contain **Klook affiliate links**. If you book through one, I may earn a commission at no extra cost to you, and affiliate links are shown in a distinct colour. Neither ads nor affiliate links affect what is written or how a place is described.
