**{{siteName}}** ("the site") takes your privacy seriously. This page explains what is collected and why.

## 1. What is collected

The site has no accounts and no sign-in. **No names, phone numbers, or other directly identifying information are collected.**

The following may be recorded automatically:

- IP address, browser type, device type, time of access
- Pages viewed and referring source

If you email via the [contact page](contact.html), your address and message are used only to reply, and are deleted afterwards.

## 2. Emoji reactions

Tapping an emoji at the bottom of a guide stores the following:

| Stored | What it is |
|---|---|
| Visitor ID | A random value (UUID) generated in your browser. It is not linked to your name, email, or IP, and cannot identify you. |
| Guide address | Which guide you reacted to |
| Emoji type | Which reaction you tapped |
| Timestamp | Used to prevent duplicate and automated voting |

- This exists **only to stop the same browser counting the same reaction twice.**
- The visitor ID is stored in your browser's `localStorage`. **Clearing your site data deletes it**, and a fresh random value is generated next time.
- Reaction records are stored with **Supabase Inc.**, whose servers may be located outside Korea.
- If you never tap a reaction, none of this is stored.

## 3. Why it's collected

- Understanding which guides are useful, so more of them get written
- Serving and measuring advertising
- De-duplicating emoji reactions
- Replying to your emails

## 4. Cookies and browser storage

The site may use cookies for analytics and advertising, and uses `localStorage` for the reaction feature.

You can block or delete cookies and site data in your browser settings. Reading the guides works exactly the same either way — you would only lose the highlight showing which reactions you had already tapped.

## 5. Advertising — Google AdSense

The site displays advertising through **Google AdSense**.

- Google and other third-party vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.
- You can opt out of personalised advertising at [Google Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads).
- To opt out of third-party vendor cookies more broadly, see [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).

For more detail, see [how Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

## 6. Analytics

The site may use Google Analytics to understand traffic. Data is aggregated and used for statistics only. You can opt out with the [browser opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
