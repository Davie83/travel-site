In Boryeong, Chungnam, Jayeon Ssambap Restaurant serves home-grown leaf vegetables, spicy pork and snail ssamjang on one table.

## Passing nearby, I suddenly remembered it — my second visit

I was passing through the area after a long time, and the restaurant came to mind by chance. This was my second visit. I remembered it as good value, with side dishes that were tasty without being salty, and that is why I went back.

## At lunchtime the inside is busy

I went at lunchtime. It was quiet outside, but inside quite a lot of people were eating.

## There are only two things to decide

The menu has a few items, but in practice you only choose between the Jayeon ssambap and the stone-pot ssambap. The Jayeon ssambap is 11,000 won and the stone-pot ssambap is 13,000 won. For the amount of food that arrives, the price is low enough to enjoy without worry **(as of Oct 2026)**.

## What arrives on the table

Spicy pork and several side dishes come out, together with the snail ssamjang and a snail doenjang stew.

This is what the table looks like in the photo, seen from above. In the middle, nine small plates sit in three rows: red-seasoned stir-fried pork, two grilled fish, seasoned greens, napa cabbage kimchi, stir-fried shiitake mushrooms with carrot, and seasoned eggplant, among others. On the right, a large plate is piled with leaf vegetables — napa cabbage leaves, frilly leaves and lettuce — and at the lower right a black stone pot holds the ssamjang with snail in it. On the left, a stew boils and steams in a black stone pot.

## Some of the leaf vegetables are grown by the restaurant

Part of the leaf vegetables is grown by the restaurant itself and brought out for the wraps.

## Gentle seasoning, and a full meal

The seasoning is not salty and leans mild. With the snail ssamjang and the snail doenjang stew as well, it was a meal that left me full. It was a return visit, and I walked out in a good mood.

## Menu words

- **Ssambap** is a meal in which you wrap rice and ssamjang in a leaf of lettuce or another leaf vegetable
- **Snail (ureongi)** here means the freshwater snail from rice paddies and waterside; the restaurant puts it in the ssamjang and the doenjang stew
- **Ssamjang** is a seasoned doenjang paste served with wraps
- **Jeyuk-bokkeum** is pork stir-fried in a gochujang-based sauce
- **Stone-pot ssambap** is ssambap with the rice served in a stone pot, 2,000 won more than the Jayeon ssambap
- To order, say "Jayeon ssambap juseyo" (자연쌈밥 주세요)

## For visitors from abroad

This is how you eat a wrap. Lay one leaf on your palm, put a little rice on it, add some ssamjang, fold it up and eat it in one bite. Spicy pork goes in well too, and you can add the ssamjang a little at a time to set the seasoning to your taste. Because the meal includes meat and snail, vegetarians should check before ordering.

## Who it suits

- ✅ You want a meal built around plenty of leaf vegetables
- ✅ You want a table with many side dishes at a price in the 10,000-won range
- ✅ You like gentle, unsalty seasoning
- ⚠️ The seasoning is mild, so if you want strong flavours it may feel plain
