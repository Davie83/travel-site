Gaemijip is a nakgopsae restaurant you can now find in Seoul too. Even so, I had only been to the Gukje Market main branch, so this time I took the chance to visit the Gwangalli main branch.

## 📍 After the Gukje Market main branch, the Gwangalli main branch

This is Gaemijip's Gwangalli main branch, near Gwangalli Beach. As far as I know, it is run by the owner's son. It is a place famous for nakgopsae.

## 🐙 The basic nakgopsae, the americano of the dish

I ordered the basic nakgopsae, which is like an americano if you think of it as coffee. Looking at the pot before it went on the heat, the seafood looked fresh.

![A round black pot holding octopus legs, shrimp and white pieces before cooking with a heap of green vegetables in the middle, surrounded by rice, dongchimi, bean sprouts, gim flakes and radish side dishes](/assets/img/busan-gaemijip-gwangalli-raw.jpg)

The photo shows the table before cooking. In a round black pot are white octopus legs and shrimp, thin flat white ovals, and white chunks dusted with something like black pepper, with a heap of green vegetables in the middle. Around the pot sit two bowls of rice, two bowls of dongchimi broth, bean sprouts, a green side dish tossed in chili flakes, a dish of radish pieces and a plate of gim flakes.

## 🥣 The dongchimi I refilled several times

The dongchimi that came out that day was so well seasoned and tasty that I had refills several times. The dongchimi is unlimited (^^).

## 🍚 Simmered down, mixed into rice, finished with gim flakes

I simmered the nakgopsae down a little, then ladled it into the bowl with the rice that came with it, letting it bubble once, and mixed it in. After mixing it all together, the gim flakes on top at the end are a must.

The main photo is the nakgopsae mid-simmer. In the black pot a red sauce is bubbling, with octopus legs, shrimp, spring onion and glass noodles showing in it. A large spoon rests across the pot, and a bean-sprout side dish peeks in at the upper left.

I tend to avoid rice because I have drinks so often in the evenings. This is a place where you cannot skip the rice, so I meant to eat just a little, yet the bowl vanished in no time. Mix various kinds of seafood and daechang (large intestine) and the like into rice, and a bowl of rice is nothing, isn't it?

## 🍺 Not a place to sit long over drinks, so one glass with the meal

I enjoy a drink, but the mood was not one for sitting long over drinks. I had about one glass with the meal and got up.

## 🌐 For visitors

- Nakgopsae (낙곱새) is named from the first syllables of nakji (small octopus), gopchang (offal) and saeu (shrimp). To order, say "낙곱새 주세요 (Nakgopsae juseyo)"
- Dongchimi (동치미) is the broth of radish water kimchi
- Gim flakes are called gimgaru (김가루), finely crumbled seaweed. Sprinkle them on after mixing it into the rice

## 👥 Who it suits

It suits people who like nakgopsae mixed into rice. It fits someone who wants one drink with the meal and then to get up, rather than a long drinking session.
