This is Jin Dwaeji Gomtang's Busan Station direct run branch, near Busan Station. The Gwangalli main branch is on the Michelin Guide, and I was curious about a gomtang made with pork instead of beef. I know there are several branches, and this time I went to the Busan Station one.

## ⏰ I went at 1 PM on purpose and waited 15 minutes

I went around 1 PM on purpose to avoid the worst of the lunch rush. As expected there was still a short line. My timing must have been good, because a crowd of diners came out together and I got in after about 15 minutes.

![Corner of a black tiled building near Busan Station with a white lettered Jin Dwaeji Gomtang Busan Station sign and glass door on the ground floor, a reddish brown Busan Station Milmyeon & Jjamppong sign on the upper floor, and three people standing out front](/assets/img/busan jin dwaeji gomtang busanstation exterior.jpg)

The photo shows the storefront. On the ground floor of the corner of a black tiled building, a white lettered sign reads "진돼지곰탕 부산역직영점", and above it a reddish brown "부산역 밀면·짬뽕" sign wraps around two sides. Diners can be seen seated through the glass door, three people stand out front, and a red square sign reading "Michelin 2026" is on the wall to the right of the entrance.

## 🪑 Two bars: kitchen side and window side

The Busan Station branch is laid out as bars. There is a bar on the kitchen side and a bar by the window, and you sit side by side at one of them.

## 🍲 Pork gomtang with warmed rice, so clean I doubted it was pork

I ordered the pork gomtang. This place serves it torem style, so unless you say otherwise the rice comes pre warmed in the broth. If you ask separately, they will bring the rice on the side.

The main photo is that day's bowl. Thin sliced pork spreads across the clear broth in a large white bowl, topped with a heap of chopped scallions, and bright green oil droplets float near the edge. Behind the bowl are a plate of kimchi, small side dishes, a small dish of red sauce and a plate of brown paste.

The broth was so clean I wondered whether it was really pork. It came with pork cut thin, with lean meat and fat in a good balance, which seems to be trending lately.

## 🥟 A bottle of soju at lunch, and a black pork dumpling I had forgotten

With food that works like a drinking snack, it felt like I should have a drink even at lunchtime, so I ordered a bottle of Daeseon soju.

![Three black pork dumplings on a white plate, a blue labelled soju bottle on the left and jars of Jin salt and Jin pepper in the back right](/assets/img/busan jin dwaeji gomtang busanstation dumpling.jpg)

Partway through the meal, the black pork dumplings I had forgotten about arrived. In the photo, three black dumplings sit on a white plate, with a blue labelled soju bottle on the left and jars labelled "진소금" and "진후추" at the back right. The dumplings had good flavor and were juicy.

The gomtang itself was not bad, but maybe I expected too much. Whether it is a limit of pork or not, it felt like neither gomtang nor classic pork gukbap, a slightly in between impression.

## 🌐 For visitors

  Gomtang is a soup of meat and bones simmered for a long time into a clear broth, served with rice. This place uses pork instead of beef. To order, say "돼지곰탕 주세요 (Dwaeji gomtang juseyo)"
  Torem is a method of warming rice by pouring hot broth over it and draining it repeatedly. Here, unless you say otherwise, the rice comes in the broth
  Jin Dwaeji Gomtang has several branches. The Gwangalli main branch is on the Michelin Guide website, and the Busan Station branch has a "Michelin 2026" sign by its entrance. The one in this review is the Busan Station branch. Check the branch name on the map before you go
  Some map apps may still show this branch as permanently closed. I confirmed in person that it was open in early November 2026

## 👥 Who it suits

For me, this is not the place I would think of when I want pork gukbap, and I am not sure I would think of it when I want gomtang either. Still, it is a direct run branch of a Michelin Guide restaurant, so it suits anyone who wants to try a clean, clear broth and the black pork dumplings without setting expectations too high. For another pork gukbap place in Busan, there is [Dumichon Dwaeji gukbap — Clean Pork Soup to Midnight](/en/posts/busan dumichon dwaejigukbap).
