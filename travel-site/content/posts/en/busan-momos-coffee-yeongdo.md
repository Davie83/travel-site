If you have ever heard of a Busan coffee place from outside Busan, it was probably Momos Coffee. After a long gap I was back in Busan and stopped by the Yeongdo branch.

## ☕ A name you may have heard from outside Busan

Momos Coffee has won many titles at various competitions. There are several branches in Busan, and this was my second visit to Momos; the first was at the main shop. This time I went to the Yeongdo branch. The entrance is a rough-textured grey wall with only the MOMOS COFFEE lettering on it, so the styling is restrained.

## 🏭 Among Yeongdo's old factories, and crowded

The interior is different from the main shop. It is a refined-looking coffee shop set among Yeongdo's old factories, yet it was bustling with people, who all seem to know about it.

![The interior of Momos Coffee Yeongdo — white pillars under a high roof frame, glass walls with roasting equipment behind, and guests crowding the order counter and the stools](/assets/img/busan-momos-coffee-yeongdo-interior.jpg)

In the photo, a dark timber roof frame rises high overhead, and behind the glass walls between white pillars you can see machines that look like coffee roasting equipment. On the left, guests sit on low stools, and in the middle people gather in front of the order counter.

## 🍋 Sour notes, so a Gesha pour-over

Since it had been a long time, and since I like sour notes, I ordered a pour-over of Gesha beans, which the shop classes as a premium bean. There was a wait, but the warm, deep acidity stimulated my sense of smell.

![A staff member handling an order inside a long white counter, with drippers on glass servers, kettles and grinders on top — the coffee bar at Momos Coffee Yeongdo](/assets/img/busan-momos-coffee-yeongdo-barista.jpg)

In the photo, a staff member handles an order behind a long white counter. On the counter are drippers set on glass servers, kettles and grinders, and trays lined up beside them hold mugs, a glass teapot, a latte and a dessert. Beyond the glass wall at the back, guests can be seen sitting.

![A white mug of Gesha pour-over beside a HAVANA bean card, with an iced drink on a tray behind it](/assets/img/busan-momos-coffee-yeongdo-gesha.jpg)

The card in the photo reads "HAVANA" and "Ethiopia Gesha Village Oma Gesha 1931 Natural Lot 140," with tasting notes of "Strawberry, Orange, Plum, Darjeeling Tea, Juicy." On the table sits a white mug of black pour-over coffee, and on a tray behind it there is also a brown iced drink.

## 🧘 Good coffee is rest in itself

When I drink good coffee, I feel healed, a sense of rest, just from the coffee itself, and I like that very much. I rested properly, and since it had been a long time, I even bought beans to take home.

## 🌐 For visitors

- Momos Coffee is a Busan coffee brand that has won many titles at competitions. There are several branches, so check on the map that you are going to the Yeongdo branch (the roastery and coffee bar)
- Pour-over means coffee brewed one cup at a time with the grounds in a filter. Gesha is a coffee variety whose name comes from the Gesha area of Ethiopia, and this shop classes it as a premium bean
- The bean card is written in English, so the bean name and tasting notes are easy to read
- You can buy beans to take home
- It is a crowded place, so expect a wait

It suits people who like coffee with acidity and want to try specialty coffee properly. Keep in mind that the place is busy and there was a wait.
