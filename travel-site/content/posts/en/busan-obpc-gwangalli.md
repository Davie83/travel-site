I was walking from Gwangalli Beach toward Marine City when the atmosphere of a pizza place made me walk in on impulse. It was not on my plan, and the spaghetti I ordered as a side turned out far better than I expected.

## 📍 On the way to Marine City, with the front wide open

You come to it on the way from Gwangalli Beach toward Marine City. I did not seek it out; I passed by, liked the mood, and went in.

![The brick-fronted building under a green awning with an OBPC PIZZA sign, and red menu posters along the wooden railing](/assets/img/busan-obpc-gwangalli-exterior.jpg)

In the photo, OBPC PIZZA is written in large letters on a white sign under a green awning, and behind the brick wall a seating area with wooden chairs opens onto the street. Red menu posters run along the wooden railing at the front, and to the right the shopfronts continue with an izakaya and a mint-green arcade.

## 🍕 Half-and-half: sobul-geuril and cream shrimp

Half-and-half pizza was possible, so I ordered sobul-geuril on one half and cream shrimp on the other. The size was smaller than the family size at an ordinary chain. Still, the two flavors each had their own character, and I enjoyed them.

The main photo is that day's table. A large pizza sits on the right of the wooden board; one half is topped with pieces of grilled meat and green chili peppers, and the other half has white sauce drizzled in a zigzag. On the left, a white oval dish holds the oven spaghetti, with tomato, peppers and pieces of meat over melted cheese, and in the middle a steel bowl holds coleslaw mixed with corn. Pickles, kimchi, two small cups of sauce, and forks and knives share the same board.

## 🍝 The side-order oven spaghetti was not an ordinary oven spaghetti

I was surprised by the oven spaghetti I ordered as a side. It came with plenty of ingredients, like a pizza, and was different from the oven spaghetti you usually get. I enjoyed it far more than I expected.

## 🥗 The coleslaw cut through the richness

I ordered coleslaw, which I like, to cut the richness. It had a quality that felt homemade.

## 🍺 Good in the daytime too, pizza and beer is a given

This place is good in the daytime as well. Pizza with beer is the truth, isn't it? In the daytime you can have a whole pizza along with a draft Red Rock, which, to my feeling, not many places serve.

## 🪟 A seat with the breakwater outside

The view outside looks like a breakwater, which felt relaxed and pleasant.

![The view out from inside OBPC Pizza, past hanging bulb lamps and skeleton decorations to a promenade, a breakwater and a bridge in the distance](/assets/img/busan-obpc-gwangalli-view.jpg)

The photo looks out from inside toward the open front. Bulb lamps and skeleton decorations hang from the ceiling, and beyond them a promenade, a bike path and the tetrapods of a breakwater line up. Under a clear sky, a bridge that looks like Gwangan Bridge stretches far off.

## 🌐 For visitors

- The menu board has a separate English menu
- Banban pija (half-and-half pizza) is a way of ordering one pizza with two flavors, half each. You can say "Banban pija-ro sobul-geuril-rang keurim-saeu juseyo" (half-and-half with sobul-geuril and cream shrimp, please)
- Oven spaghetti is spaghetti topped with cheese and other ingredients and baked in the oven. The board out front lists it as "oven pasta"
- Coleslaw is a salad of shredded cabbage that balances the richness of the pizza
- "Pimaek" (pi-maek) is the Korean word for pizza and beer together

## 👥 Who it suits

If you expect a big family-size pizza, this one may feel small. It suits people who want to try two flavors in one pizza, and people who want a light daytime pizza and draft beer.

I have not decided when I will be back in Busan. Still, if I find myself in Gwangalli, I would like to have a simple pizza and draft beer here in the daytime. For another Gwangalli read, there is [Gwangalli Beach — Staying a Day Longer for the Fireworks](/en/posts/busan-gwangalli-fireworks).
