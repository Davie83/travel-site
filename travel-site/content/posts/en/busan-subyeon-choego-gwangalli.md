I had heard that this place made the Top 3 at a pork soup contest held in Busan this year, so it was the first place I stopped at when I came down to Busan this time. It is Subyeon Choego Dwaeji-gukbap, and the branch I went to is the Gwangalli one.

## 🏆 A Top 3 finisher at this year's Busan Pork Soup Festival

At the 2026 Busan Pork Soup Festival (2026 부산돼지국밥대전), the Millak main branch of Subyeon Choego Dwaeji-gukbap made the Top 3. Busan city announced the result in October from the votes of more than 38,000 people, sorted into Top 3, Top 10 and Top 20, and it did not rank the places within the Top 3. Yeongjin Dwaeji-gukbap's main branch and Hapcheon Gukbapjip were also named in the Top 3.

The place I went to is the Gwangalli branch, not the main branch that made the Top 3. The contest result belongs to the main branch, so please read this as a review of a branch with the same name.

## 📍 The Gwangalli branch, under three minutes from my lodging, instead of the main branch

I left at dawn and reached the Gwangalli branch early in the morning. When I go somewhere new, I tend to visit the main branch first, wherever it is. But I was tired from the trip down, and the Gwangalli branch was less than three minutes from my lodging, so this time I was lazy and came here.

Even though I went early in the morning, there were quite a few customers. More than half of the tables were taken.

## 🪑 Clean inside, as if it had opened recently

The Gwangalli branch seemed to have opened recently, because the interior was very clean from the start.

![Inside the shop, with a wood-slat wall, brown pendant lamps, stainless steel tables, benches with red cushions and a black wall bearing the Subyeon Choego Dwaeji-gukbap logo](/assets/img/busan-subyeon-choego-gwangalli-interior.jpg)

In the photo, a wood-slat wall and brown pendant lamps hanging from the ceiling stand out. Wooden benches with red cushions sit between stainless steel tables, and stacks of paper cups stand on the tables like towers. The black wall in the middle carries the red logo and the words "수변최고 돼지국밥", and at the back right a kitchen with a stainless steel refrigerator can be seen.

## 🍖 Suyuk for a morning meal, with a pot of broth each

I was not hungry that early in the morning, so I ordered suyuk. They also brought out the pork-soup broth, one pot per person. I thought that was impressive.

The main photo is that day's table. Thinly sliced suyuk is layered on a black plate in the middle, with round slices of sundae (순대, Korean sausage) at one end of the plate. Pots of white broth topped with spring onion sit around it. A dish of thinly cut chives, another with garlic, chili peppers and onion, lettuce, kimchi and radish kimchi, and two small dishes of red seasoning are on the same table.

## 🥣 Thick, I expected, but light like seolleongtang

I put in plenty of spring onion and tasted the broth first. When I first saw it I thought it would be very thick, but unlike what I expected, the broth was light, with a feel like seolleongtang. It was quite far from the taste I had imagined.

The seasoning was just right, and I felt like ordering a bowl of rice to add a little. It tasted good, and I thought it was no accident that it placed in this year's contest.

## 🌐 For visitors

- Dwaeji-gukbap (돼지국밥) is Busan's signature gukbap, rice served in pork broth
- Suyuk (수육) is boiled pork, thinly sliced. To order, say "수육 주세요 (Suyuk juseyo)"
- Search the map for "수변최고돼지국밥 광안점". There are separate main, Centum and Mipo branches with the same name, so check which branch you are heading to
- The Top 3 finisher is the main branch. The place in this post is the Gwangalli branch

## 👥 Who it suits

It suits people who heard about the Busan pork soup contest and want to try the place with that name, and people who like a light broth rather than a thick one. If I visit again, I want to go to the main branch and order the dwaeji-gukbap instead of suyuk. It was a good meal. For another Busan pork soup place, there is [Dumichon Dwaeji-gukbap — Clean Pork Soup to Midnight](/en/posts/busan-dumichon-dwaejigukbap).
