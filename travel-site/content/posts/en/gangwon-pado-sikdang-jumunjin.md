**This is a place that comes to mind as soon as the weather turns cold.** It's in Jumunjin.

## Why bokjiri instead of cod soup

Most people here seem to order the cod soup. But I figure **Seoul already has plenty of decent cod soup spots**, so I don't feel the need to come all the way to Jumunjin for it. Instead, I come here when I'm craving **bokjiri** — something comparatively harder to find back in Seoul.

![Grilled fish at Pado Sikdang — two whole fish grilled and served with sauce and side dishes](/assets/img/gangwon-pado-sikdang-jumunjin-saengseongui.jpg)

## I had bokjiri and grilled fish

This time I ordered **bokjiri and grilled fish**. Bokjiri is a clear, light pufferfish soup — not spicy, with a refreshing broth. I paired it with the grilled fish on the side.

## What it looks like

The bokjiri is at a rolling boil in the pot. Through the pale froth you can see pieces of white-fleshed fish, other white ingredients and thick-cut green onion, with a ladle resting in the pot. The broth is on the clear side, with no chili stirred in.

## What is bokjiri?

- **Bokeo** is pufferfish. It is poisonous, so in Korea it must be prepared by someone with a pufferfish-handling qualification
- **Jiri** is a clear fish soup made without chili powder, and **bokjiri** is jiri made with pufferfish
- It is not spicy and tastes clean and refreshing, so it works for people who find spicy food hard

## Most of the menu is good, and it feels home-cooked

**Most of what's on the menu tastes good.** It's not an elaborate spread, but there's something about it that feels like a meal made at home. The side dishes are simple and well put together, and it's an easy, comfortable meal overall.

## A place to return to when it gets colder

**With the weather turning colder, I'm planning to come back once it drops further.** A clear, warm broth like bokjiri pairs especially well with cold weather.

## For foreign visitors

- **Bokjiri is pufferfish simmered in a clear broth.** It's mild and not spicy, so it's an easy dish to try
- **The grilled fish** comes whole, grilled and served with a dipping sauce
- Pufferfish requires careful, licensed preparation to be safe to eat — this is a long-running specialist restaurant, so you can order with confidence

## Who this is for

- ✅ Anyone who likes **bokjiri** — a clear, refreshing broth
- ✅ People looking for something **harder to find back home** than cod soup
- ✅ Anyone who wants a **home-cooked-feeling meal** without needing it to be elaborate
- ✅ Anyone wanting to warm up as the weather turns cold
- ⚠️ Closed every Monday, so check the day before you go
- ⚠️ There's a break time (2:00–5:00 PM), so plan your visit around it
