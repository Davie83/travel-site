In Daegot-myeon, Gimpo, Gyeonggi, Yaksan Sondubu is a tofu specialist close to Chojidaegyo Bridge, the way onto Ganghwa Island.

## Close to the bridge, on the way to Ganghwa

It stands near Chojidaegyo Bridge to Ganghwa Island. By the way the districts are drawn, it belongs to Daegot-myeon in Gimpo rather than to Ganghwa. It sits just before you cross over to the island, so it is easy to stop on the way.

## After a long while, for a breakfast

I was passing by and wanted a bite for breakfast, so I stopped in for the first time in a while.

## The menu is sundubu and hotpot

White sundubu, red sundubu and tofu hotpot are the main dishes. This time I ordered the red sundubu.

## How the red sundubu arrives

It comes with a few side dishes.

This is what the table looks like in the photo. On the right, a black earthenware pot is bubbling hard, with sundubu in an orange broth topped with red chili oil. On the left, white rice sits in a steel bowl. Above them are six small dishes: cubed radish kimchi, white radish pieces, a dark-coloured side dish, a seaweed salad, seasoned bean sprouts and seasoned greens.

The broth tastes spicy and not spicy at the same time. I like quite spicy food, so if you come expecting heat, it may feel a little gentle.

## Sundubu is the right breakfast

Sundubu feels like a good fit for breakfast. Whatever you call it, I left with my stomach filled by the soft, wobbly curds. If you are heading to Ganghwa Island early in the day, it is worth a stop.

## Menu words

- **Sundubu** is a stew made from soft tofu that has not been pressed into blocks
- **Red sundubu** generally means sundubu simmered red with chili seasoning, while **white sundubu** is the clear version without it
- **Dubu-jeongol** is a hotpot of tofu and vegetables cooked at the table
- **Sondubu** means tofu made by hand
- To order, say "Ppalgan sundubu juseyo" (빨간순두부 주세요)

## For visitors from abroad

The sundubu arrives still boiling in its earthenware pot, so let it cool a little before you eat so you do not burn your mouth. The rice comes separately, and you can put it into the broth or eat it alongside the side dishes. If you are coming by car, the wide parking area in front is handy.

## Who it suits

- ✅ You want something warm and easy to eat early in the morning
- ✅ You need a meal stop on the way to Ganghwa Island
- ✅ You like soft, wobbly sundubu
- ⚠️ If you expect a lot of heat, it may feel mild
