**This is a restaurant that specializes in uni (sea urchin).** The main dish is the uni rice bowl, often called uni-don in the Japanese style.

## The main dish is the uni rice bowl, plus bibimbap and seaweed soup

The center of the menu is the **uni rice bowl (seonggedeopbap).** There are also **sea urchin bibimbap** and **seaweed soup.** It seems there's a branch in Hamdeok too, but I couldn't confirm it, and this post is about the Aewol branch.

## Small and large, by grams

The uni-don is **split into small and large by the grams of uni, and the price differs.** As I remember it, the large was 80 g and the uni platter (uni hanpan) was 100 g (check the exact grams before you go). As of Oct 2026 the price is 29,000 KRW for small and 39,000 KRW for large.

## Even the large wasn't enough, but the uni was sweet

For someone who loves uni, even the large **didn't feel like enough.** Still, I enjoyed **uni that was sweet rather than salty,** more or less. If you eat a lot of uni, factor the portion into your order.

## Two things I wished were different

- They gave **only one sheet of gamtae.** I was so disappointed that I asked for more, and was told extra has to be ordered separately
- **They don't sell alcohol.** If you want a drink with your uni, you'll need another plan

## Taking the uni platter to go

Still hungry, I **took the uni platter to go.** I needed a drink. But **uni spoils easily, so you have to get back and eat it quickly.** My steps got faster.

## Before you go

- It's open **9 AM–7:30 PM**, with a break around 2:30–4 PM (as of Oct 2026; sources differ slightly on the times, so check before you go)
- No alcohol is sold, and takeout is available
- Parking is available

## A note for international visitors

- **Uni** is the Japanese name for sea urchin roe, and **uni-don** is a rice bowl topped with it. On the Korean menu it's **seonggedeopbap**
- **Gamtae** is a fine, soft green seaweed eaten with rice. This shop gives only one sheet, and extra has to be ordered
- Sizes are **small and large,** with different grams of uni. I found even the large too little
- Alcohol isn't sold

## Who it's for

- ✅ People who want to eat **uni (sea urchin)** properly
- ✅ People who like **sweet uni** that isn't salty
- ⚠️ People who want to eat **a lot** of uni — even the large may leave you wanting more
- ⚠️ People planning to have alcohol with the meal — none is sold
- ⚠️ People who want plenty of gamtae — extra must be ordered
