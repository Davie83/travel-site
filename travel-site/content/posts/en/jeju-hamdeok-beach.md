**This is Hamdeok Beach on Jeju's northeast coast.** The sea really is a lovely emerald color.

## It's fairly touristy now

With so many places to stay nearby, it **feels fairly touristy these days.** The terrace building jutting out toward the water in the photo below is a cafe too (Cafe Delmoondo).

![The view over Hamdeok Beach — emerald water, with a seaside cafe (Cafe Delmoondo) along the shore](/assets/img/jeju-hamdeok-beach-view.jpg)

## Seowubong is right there for a walk

**Seowubong is right next to the beach**, so it's easy to keep walking after you've looked at the sea.

## People on month-long stays

I saw quite a few **Koreans and foreigners doing month-long stays** here. There's a lot of accommodation, and the beach, cafes, and walking paths are all close together, so it seems like a place people settle into. **It's on my own list of places I'd like to try a month-long stay someday.**

## For foreign visitors

- **Hamdeok** is the name of a village on Jeju's northeast coast (administratively Jocheon-eup, Jeju City), and the beach takes its name from it
- An **oreum** is one of the small volcanic hills scattered across Jeju. Seowubong is one of them, and being right next to the beach it makes an easy, light walk
- Swimming is safest during the **official beach season**. At other times, stick to walking and enjoying the scenery

## Who this is for

- ✅ Anyone who wants to **walk along** Jeju's emerald water
- ✅ Anyone who wants to pair a beach stroll with a **walk up Seowubong**
- ✅ Anyone researching a Jeju month-long stay who wants to **get a feel for the place**
- ⚠️ If you're expecting a quiet, empty beach — with so many cafes and places to stay, it has a touristy feel
- ⚠️ If swimming is the goal, check the official beach season dates

> This is a first-hand account, and impressions of the scenery are subjective. Hours and facilities are **(as of Oct 2026)** — worth checking again before you go.
