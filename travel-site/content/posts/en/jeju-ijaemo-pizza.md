**Lee Jaemo Pizza, the one famous in Busan, has a branch in Jeju too.** The sign reads SINCE 1992. I've been to the Busan shop as well, and eating it in Jeju I got the same impression: **a classic pizza that sticks to the basics.** I rarely get to Busan these days, so when it crosses my mind in Jeju, I stop by.

![Lee Jaemo Pizza Jeju from the outside — a green building with a SINCE 1992 sign](/assets/img/jeju-ijaemo-pizza-store.jpg)

## 🧀 Cheese first, cheese crust as the default

The **cheese here is really good.** It's full of flavor. The default menu item is the **cheese crust pizza**, a thick pizza with cheese in the edge dough.

## 🍕 It comes on a black pan

On the black pan, the pizza is topped with pepperoni, ham, green pepper and mushrooms, with the cheese browned in spots. The edge is a thick, puffed cheese crust, and a handled server for lifting slices comes with the pan. Beside it sit a salad plate with fruit, a red iced drink, and a small bowl of cheese sauce topped with crispy fried onion.

## 📏 Large over small

Since the **price gap between small and large isn't big, I recommend the large** (large 30,000 KRW, small 26,000 KRW as of Oct 2026). I'm someone who can't finish even one pizza, but with so little price difference, I think ordering the large is the better call. I've heard the kimchi fried rice is good too, but since I can't even finish the pizza, I haven't tried it yet.

## ⏰ A notice says it's closed on Sundays

It opens at 10 AM and last order is 8:30 PM (as of Oct 2026). A notice says it's closed on Sundays, so check once more before you go. Parking is available.

## 🌐 A note for international visitors

- To order, say **"Chijeu-keureoseuteu pija raji hana juseyo"** (one large cheese crust pizza, please)
- The flavor is classic and back-to-basics, so it may feel ordinary if you're expecting flashy toppings or something new

## 👥 Who it's for

It's for people who've heard of Busan's Lee Jaemo Pizza and want to try it in Jeju, people who like a classic pizza with rich-tasting cheese, and anyone looking for one large pizza to share between two or more.
