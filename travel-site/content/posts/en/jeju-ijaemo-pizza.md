**Lee Jaemo Pizza, famous in Busan, has a branch in Jeju too.** The sign reads SINCE 1992.

## Not just Busan — Jeju too

This is the very same Lee Jaemo Pizza that's famous in Busan. I've been to the Busan shop too, and eating it in Jeju I got the same impression: **a classic pizza that sticks to the basics.** I rarely get to Busan these days, so when it crosses my mind in Jeju, I stop by.

![Lee Jaemo Pizza Jeju from the outside — a green building with a SINCE 1992 sign](/assets/img/jeju-ijaemo-pizza-store.jpg)

## The cheese comes first

The **cheese here is really good.** It's full of flavor. The default menu item is the **cheese crust pizza**.

## Large over small

Since the **price gap between small and large isn't big, I recommend the large** (large 30,000 KRW, small 26,000 KRW as of Oct 2026). I'm someone who can't finish even one pizza, but with so little price difference, I think ordering the large is the better call.

## What it looks like

On the black pan, the pizza is topped with pepperoni, ham, green pepper and mushrooms, with the cheese browned in spots. The edge is a thick, puffed cheese crust, and a handled server for lifting slices comes with the pan. Beside it sit a salad plate with fruit, a red iced drink, and a small bowl of cheese sauce topped with crispy fried onion.

## Before you go

- The default menu item is the **cheese crust pizza**
- I've heard the kimchi fried rice is good too, but I can't even finish the pizza, so I haven't tried it yet
- It opens at 10 AM and last order is 8:30 PM (as of Oct 2026). A notice says it's closed on Sundays, so check once more before you go
- Parking is available

## A note for international visitors

- **Lee Jaemo Pizza** is a pizza shop famous in Busan, and this Jeju branch's sign reads SINCE 1992
- **Cheese crust** means a thick pizza with cheese in the edge dough. It's this shop's default menu item
- There are two sizes, **small (S) and large (L)**, with little price difference, so if you're a group the large is the better choice
- The flavor is classic and back-to-basics, so it may feel ordinary if you're expecting flashy toppings or something new

## Who it's for

- ✅ People who've heard of Busan's Lee Jaemo Pizza and **want to try it in Jeju**
- ✅ People who like a **classic pizza** with rich-tasting cheese
- ✅ People looking for **one large pizza** to share between two or more
- ⚠️ People hoping for unusual toppings and new flavors
- ⚠️ People planning to go on a Sunday — a notice says it's closed
