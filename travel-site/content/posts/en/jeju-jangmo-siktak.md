**In Ido-dong, Jeju City, Jangmo Siktak serves abalone stone-pot rice, a stew and soy-marinated crab on one table, and the value is excellent.** It was my first meal on this trip to Jeju.

## Why it was my first meal in Jeju

Measured from Jeju Airport, it is inside the city but a little to the south-east. It is good value, so **you are told to expect a wait at mealtimes.** I arrived at an in-between hour and was lucky enough to be seated straight away.

## The stone-pot rice is the one I recommend

![The full table at Jangmo Siktak — abalone stone-pot rice, a clear soup, a red stew, soy-marinated crab, marinated abalone, stir-fried pork, salad and side dishes on a wooden table](/assets/img/jeju-jangmo-siktak-stew.jpg)

**I would dare to recommend the abalone stone-pot rice.** There is more abalone than I expected, and the innards — called *geu-u* — are well mixed into the rice. If you scorch the leftover rice into nurungji in the pot, **the flavour of the innards comes through more strongly than I expected.**

## What arrives on the table

The soy-marinated crab also comes in a bigger portion than I expected, and the crab is **hwang-ge (황게)**. You can choose the stew to suit your taste. Marinated abalone, stir-fried pork, a salad and side dishes arrive with it. **For one meal in Jeju, the value of this spread is very good.**

The basic side dishes are ordinary, but this is a place where you taste several main-course dishes at one table. The **Jangmo set is 17,000 won**. Google Maps shows the price range as **10,000–20,000 won per person** (as of Oct 2026).

## Finding it

The address is **51 Yeonsin-ro, Ido-dong, Jeju City**, listed as in front of Hanmaeum Hospital. A travel guide site lists the Jeon Isu Gallery about 450 m away as a nearby stop. Parking is available.

It is open **9:00 AM to 8:30 PM** with last order at 7:40 PM, and **closed on Sundays** (as of Oct 2026).

## For visitors from abroad

- **A set meal (*jeongsik*, 定食)** puts rice, stew and side dishes on one table. The signature here is the **Jangmo set (장모정식)**
- **Abalone stone-pot rice** is rice cooked in a heavy stone pot with abalone on top. ***Geu-u*** is the abalone's innards; it seeps into the rice and gives a deep flavour
- **Ganjang gejang** is raw crab marinated in soy sauce. Because it is raw, it divides opinion
- There are several stews and **you choose one when you order.** Heat varies by stew, so ask the staff
