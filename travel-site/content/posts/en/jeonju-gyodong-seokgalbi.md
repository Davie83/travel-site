Walking around Jeonju Hanok Village, there's a point where you start to get hungry. That's exactly when this place caught my eye.

## A table set for the eyes first

I ordered seokgalbi, and even though it's a single hot-plate dish, the table filled up with side dishes. Japchae, acorn jelly, wild garlic, sweet pumpkin porridge — well over a dozen colorful little plates. It's a spread you can enjoy just by looking at it.

![The full spread at Gyodong Seokgalbi — the sizzling stone-plate seokgalbi surrounded by a dozen-plus side dishes](/assets/img/jeonju-gyodong-seokgalbi-table.jpg)

## The taste? It was... fine

To be honest, the taste itself was just average. Not bad, but not something to rave about either — it was **just fine, perfectly edible**. Still, between the number of side dishes and the lively, colorful spread, I left full and in a good mood.

Go in without setting your expectations too high, more as a solid meal break in the middle of touring the hanok village, and it fits the bill well.

## Whichever way you walk out, there's a path

Once you finish and step out, you're in the hanok village, so wherever you walk next, pretty alleys and walking paths carry on from there. You don't really need a set destination — just follow your feet.

## Good to know

- **Seokgalbi** is marinated galbi grilled sizzling on a hot stone plate. It's not spicy.
- It comes with **a dozen-plus side dishes** — part of the fun is trying each one.
- It's **inside the hanok village**, so it's an easy, walkable stop for visitors.
- Keep expectations moderate — the spread is more memorable than the flavor itself.

## Before you go

- **Location** — 26 Taejo-ro, Wansan-gu, Jeonju, inside Jeonju Hanok Village.
- **Hours** — Daily 11:00 AM–8:30 PM (break 3:30–4:00 PM), said to be open year-round.
- **Price** — 15,000 won for seokgalbi.
- **Route** — Good for a hunger break mid-way through touring the hanok village. Whichever alley you take afterward, there's a walking path.
- **Nearby** — [Jeonju Waengi Kongnamul-gukbap](/en/posts/jeonju-waengi-gukbap) is about a 5-minute walk away. Good to pair for the next morning or when you need a hangover-soup fix.

> Based on a personal visit. Taste is a matter of personal preference, and prices and hours can change — check again before you go.
