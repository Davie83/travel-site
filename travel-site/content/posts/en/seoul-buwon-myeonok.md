In summer I have always had to wait. Buwon Myeonok is a naengmyeon restaurant on the second floor of Namdaemun Market, and the waiting happens on a narrow staircase.

## 🪜 You wait on the narrow stairs

I have been about five times, and this time I went on a weekend. I haven't been when it is cold, so I can't say, but in summer there has always been a wait. Because the restaurant is on the second floor, you wait on the narrow stairs between the first and second floors. The address is 41-6 Namdaemunsijang 4-gil, 2nd floor, and in the market alley it is easy to look only at the ground floor and walk past, so it is quicker to follow a map. Enter Namdaemun Market from Hoehyeon Station (Line 4).

## 🍜 A basic Pyeongyang naengmyeon with a slight twist

Pyeongyang naengmyeon is buckwheat noodles served in a cold broth. Many people think it "tastes like nothing" the first time, because the broth is clear and not strongly seasoned. You eat it for the buckwheat aroma and the grain of the broth, not for a punch. This place leans toward the mild, old-school style rather than today's richer versions. To my taste it feels like a basic Pyeongyang naengmyeon with something slightly added, a little fusion that isn't Hamhung naengmyeon, and the noodles are just how I like them.

In the photo, a metal bowl is full of clear broth with brown-tinged buckwheat noodles loosened in it. On top sit half a boiled egg, sliced meat, pieces of cucumber and radish, and bok choy leaves. If it tastes too mild, add the mustard and vinegar on the table a little at a time.

## 🥞 Bindaetteok and chicken salad

Bindaetteok is a savoury pancake of ground mung beans fried with meat or vegetables. Here it is fried in meat fat, so it is very rich and nutty. It is small, but the price is fair and it tastes good, and it balances the mild noodles. I have also had the chicken salad (dak-muchim), which is generous in portion and works as a snack with drinks. At other tables I sometimes see the spicy pork salad (jeyuk-muchim) going out. At the top of the photo, two golden pancakes sit on a plate, with a soy sauce dish, white pickled radish and a brass cup, the traditional kind used for makgeolli and other drinks.

## 🧽 Clean, for a building inside a market

It's a building inside a market, so it isn't spotless, but compared with other shops it looks like they care about cleanliness.

## 🌐 For visitors

- It is a traditional place with a short menu, so simply pointing is enough
- Cards are standard in Seoul, but if you will browse the market too, bring some cash
- If it is your first Pyeongyang naengmyeon, it can taste bland. Know that this place is on the "traditional" side

## 👥 Two or four people works well

Two or four people seems about right. It suits people looking for old-style Pyeongyang naengmyeon, those who want a nutty bindaetteok alongside, and anyone with a Namdaemun Market day planned. It's a good lunch stop mid-shopping, but it's on the second floor so the entrance is easy to miss, and it closes at 8 PM (as of Aug 2026), so leave plenty of time if you go late. If the day works, you could also compare it with [Maknae Hoejip](/en/posts/seoul-maknae-hoejip) in the same market, which serves its sashimi set only at weekday lunch.
