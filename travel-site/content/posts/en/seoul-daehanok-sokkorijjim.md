**If you're after an old-school ox-tail place in Yeongdeungpo, this is it.** I drop by every so often, and one thing is consistent — there is almost always a wait at meal times.

## Waiting is part of the deal

**Whether it's lunch or dinner, expect to wait if you go during a meal rush.** This is a veteran restaurant with a name that draws a steady crowd.

If a line sounds like a hassle, aim for an off-peak slot, roughly 2–4 PM.

## Tender suyuk, chives, and a milky bone broth

Order, and **ox-tail suyuk arrives with seasoned chives on top.** The meat is boiled until properly tender, and a sharply seasoned pile of chives covers it. Alongside comes a **milky bone broth.**

The real move here comes after you've finished the meat. **Once the meat is gone, mix thin wheat noodles into the leftover chive seasoning on the plate** — that's the proper way to finish this meal. Skip it and you've only eaten half the point of coming.

- **Don't worry about spice.** The chives are sharp with garlic and sesame, not fiery hot
- **Don't worry about a strong meaty smell either.** It's boiled well, so any gaminess is minimal

## Prices seem to keep climbing

**Honestly, every time I go, it feels like prices have crept up again.** This isn't a cheap old-school spot. I've noted the current figures per Diningcode, but prices change often enough that it's worth checking the menu again on-site.

## The portion, though, always feels a touch short

The flavor is genuinely good. But **the ox-tail suyuk portion always feels a bit small relative to the price** — that impression hasn't gone away over multiple visits. It's more noticeable when splitting with a group, so on a hungrier day, order a larger size or add another dish.

## It's close to Yeouido too

**If you have plans around West Yeouido or the National Assembly, this is walkable.** It's a different flavor of place than Yeouido's own lunch-hour veterans — better suited to an evening with a drink.

The nearest subway station is **Yeongdeungpo Station (Line 1)**, about a 10-minute walk from Exit 5. If spicy jjamppong sounds better than ox-tail that day, [Songjukjang](/en/posts/seoul-songjukjang) is also walkable, in the same Yeongdeungpo neighborhood.

## Checklist before you go

- **Closed Sundays and Mondays.** Confirmed via Diningcode
- **Closes at 8:40 PM (last order 7:40 PM).** Leave enough time if it's a late dinner
- **A wait at meal times is standard.** Aim for an off-peak slot or budget extra time
- **Prices keep shifting.** Worth a re-check on the menu board
