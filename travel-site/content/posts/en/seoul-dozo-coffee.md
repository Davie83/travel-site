A calm place for a proper cup. This is where I go when I want **single-origin filter coffee** rather than the usual americano.

## Korean specialty coffee, briefly

Seoul has an unusually deep specialty coffee scene — small independent roasters everywhere, brewing single-origin beans by hand. **If your idea of Korean coffee is a chain iced americano, this is the other end of it.**

This is one of those places: a short bean list, filter brewed to order, and beans roasted for you to take home.

## The way it arrives is the point

The coffee comes **in a stemmed tulip glass on a walnut tray**, with the remainder in a small steel kettle beside it and a card describing the day's beans.

This isn't just presentation. **The narrow glass concentrates the aroma**, and filter coffee changes noticeably as it cools — fruit acidity forward when hot, sweeter and rounder as it drops. Pouring from the kettle in stages lets you actually taste that arc.

So treat it as **a cup to sit with**, not something to take away.

## Pick your time — this matters

Being straight with you: **the room is small**, and this is an **office district**.

- **Weekday lunch and afternoon** — fills with people working nearby. Not a quiet spot for one
- **Weekends** — reported as noticeably calmer. This is when to come if you want to linger

It is **not a laptop cafe.** Come for one good cup, then move on.

## Where it is

The address is **134 Tongil-ro, Jongno-gu.** It sits near the site of **Donuimun**, the old west gate of the city, and is a short walk from **Seodaemun Station (Line 5).**

It fits neatly into a walk around **Donuimun Museum Village and Gyeonghuigung Palace** — a reasonable coffee stop in the middle of that route, away from the Insadong and Bukchon crowds.

> **Dozo has more than one branch** — there is another inside The Hyundai Seoul in Yeouido. Search for **"Donuimun"** specifically, or you will end up across the river.

## Set expectations honestly

The Google rating is 4.7, but that is **from three reviews.** The shop opened only a few months ago, so there isn't a real track record yet. Don't read that number as "verified."

Prices start at **₩5,000**, rising with the bean and the cup you choose. That is roughly chain-cafe territory at the bottom end, which makes the tray service good value.

## Practical notes

- **Payment** — card is standard everywhere in Seoul, including small cafes
- **Ordering** — a short menu; pointing at the bean list works
- **Takeaway** — possible, but the tray service is the reason to come, so drink in if you can

## Who this suits

- ✅ You might buy beans to take home
- ✅ You're walking the Donuimun / Gyeonghuigung / Seodaemun area
- ⚠️ Small room, busy with office workers on weekdays
