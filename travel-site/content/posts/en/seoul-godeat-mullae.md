**If you want tacos, the set I ordered makes a generous meal on its own.**

## One set is enough for two

I had the **fajita & taco set (for two)**. It arrives exactly as in the photo.

- **Cast-iron pan, right** — the fajita: prawns and meat, grilled shishito peppers and cherry tomatoes, rice topped with sour cream, all in one pan
- **Board, left** — tacos, already assembled with meat and vegetables on tortillas
- **Small bowls in the middle** — guacamole, sour cream, salsa, coriander and lime
- Plus fries and extra tortillas

**You do not need to order anything else.** If you have come for tacos and do not know what to pick, this set is the answer.

**The food is good and the room is pleasant.** It goes well with beer.

## Mullae has changed

This is the part I want to say as much as anything about the restaurant.

**There are far more young people in Mullae now than there used to be, and foreign visitors too.**

Mullae was a district of **small steel workshops**. They still run during the day — and in between them, studios, cafes and bars have moved into the old buildings. The area is called **Mullae Art Village**.

So this is a neighbourhood where **it would be a waste to eat and leave.** Walk the alleys after your meal.

### Why it is worth a short stop

**Mullae Station is on Line 2**, the loop line that circles Seoul. **Hongdae, Euljiro 1-ga, Gangnam and Jamsil all sit on that same line.**

That means **no transfer**. From Mullae it is roughly 5 km in a straight line to Hongdae, 9 km to Euljiro 1-ga and 12 km to Gangnam.

**It barely takes you off your main route, so if you want to see a place where the old and the young sit side by side, it is worth a short stop.** Neighbourhoods where steel workshops and artist studios share one alley are not common, even in Seoul.

The restaurant is **510 m from exit 7 of Mullae Station, a 9-minute walk**.

## For international visitors

**This is not Korean food** — it is Mexican. But it makes an easy meal if you are in Mullae, so it is worth noting.

- **Barely spicy.** The heat is in the **salsa served on the side**, so you decide. Skip it and nothing is spicy
- **Coriander comes separately.** If you dislike it, simply leave it out — it is not mixed in beforehand
- **Tacos are eaten with your hands.** Fold the tortilla
- **The fajita pan is extremely hot.** Do not touch it
- **The tortillas are wheat flour.** Not suitable if you avoid gluten
- **There are prawns.** Mention it before ordering if you have a shellfish allergy
- **One set feeds two.** You do not need to order per person
- **On weekdays there is a break from 3:00 to 5:00 PM.** A late lunch will not work (there is no break on Saturday or Sunday)
- **The weekday lunch menu stops at 2:10 PM** (except public holidays)
