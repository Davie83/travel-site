I have been to all three Hadongkwan branches, and the one I have gone to most is the Myeongdong main branch. This post is written around the day I went to the Yeouido branch.

## 🏮 Each branch has its own feel

Hadongkwan has branches in Myeongdong and Yeouido, and Yeouido has two, which is confusing. When you look it up on a map, **check the branch name too.**

- **Myeongdong main branch** — near Euljiro 1-ga Station and Myeongdong Cathedral, so it fits a sightseeing route, and it is where I have gone most
- **Yeouido Direct-Run Branch 1** — Exit 3 of National Assembly Station. This is the centre of Hadongkwan in Yeouido, and where I went this time
- **Yeouido Station branch** — on Yeouinaru-ro

The Myeongdong main branch and the Yeouido branch I visited this time are a little old-fashioned inside, while the Yeouido Station branch is a stylish modern shop.

## 🥣 Clear-broth gomtang

Gomtang is a soup made by simmering beef and bones for a long time. Many people picture a milky broth when they hear gomtang, but that is the seolleongtang side, and this place is on the clear side. It isn't heavily seasoned and the broth itself comes first, so the first spoonful can seem bland, and that is the direction of this dish. Salt and pepper are on the table to adjust to taste, and the rice already comes inside the soup, in a gukbap style.

In the photo, a bowl with a brassy sheen holds clear broth, with chopped green onion heaped on top. Rice grains and pieces of beef show through the broth, and a spoon and chopsticks rest across the bowl. In the brass dish on the left are kkakdugi and napa cabbage kimchi. The broth is clear rather than greasy. A brass bowl is a traditional vessel made of brass.

## 🥒 Kkakdugi brine turns it into a different gomtang

This is the most important thing about the place, and the restaurant also explains this way of eating. After eating a fair amount of the clear broth, **ask for more kkakdugi brine** and pour it in. The colour and flavour change, and it feels like eating a different kind of gomtang. It moves toward sour and refreshing. It is two flavours in one bowl, so don't add it from the start; taste the clear broth properly first. Kkakdugi is kimchi made from cubed radish, and it is the other pillar of this place after the gomtang.

## ⏰ Around 11:30 for lunch, a moderate wait

I mostly went at lunch, and at around 11:30 I either waited a moderate while or went straight in. It opens early in the morning, since gomtang is traditionally a morning food, and early hours are relaxed. Food arrives within a minute of ordering. Service is businesslike; it is not a place for friendly hospitality but an old-school shop where you finish a bowl quickly and leave. It also closes early, sometimes even earlier when the ingredients run out, so hurry if you plan to go after lunch. If you will still be in Yeouido in the evening, you could have pho for dinner at [Beyond Vietnam, National Assembly branch](/en/posts/seoul-beyond-vietnam) in the same neighbourhood.

## 💸 Prices keep rising, and the broth may not be hot

The basic gomtang and the "25" are clearly expensive for a bowl of gukbap. At Hadongkwan the price becomes the menu name, so "25" means the 25,000 KRW bowl, and you can check in the shop what changes at each level. The way prices keep rising is a drawback. Some people may feel the portion is small for the price even though the taste is good, so it is worth the money if you want an old shop that does Korean-beef gomtang properly, and not the place if you want budget gukbap.

Another point is temperature. It is a toryeom-style gukbap (rice warmed by pouring hot broth over it), so the broth doesn't arrive at a rolling boil. If you dislike toryeom-style gukbap, you may find it on the cool side.

## 🌐 For visitors

- To order, say "Gomtang juseyo" (곰탕 주세요), meaning "one gomtang, please"
- It isn't spicy. It is a soup of beef and bones, so it isn't suitable for vegetarians
- You ask for the kkakdugi brine partway through. There is a notice in the shop

It suits people looking for an old shop that does clear-broth gomtang properly, those who want to try two flavours with the kkakdugi brine, and anyone who needs a hearty bowl for breakfast. Bear in mind that service is businesslike, turnover is fast, and it is pricey by gukbap standards.
