**In Yeongdeungpo, Seoul, Hao'sum on basement level 1 of Times Square is a Hong Kong-style restaurant that specialises in dim sum.** I understand it has several branches, and this time I went to the one in the basement of Times Square in Yeongdeungpo.

## At a Chinese restaurant, I always have to think

Whenever I sit down in a Chinese restaurant I agonise over the order. Dim sum is a given, but among that many dishes, which one should be the dish that anchors today's meal? I spend a good while on it.

That day I settled on **soup dumplings, black har gow and stir-fried water spinach**, and I also ordered **a Hong Kong-style clay-pot rice with pork and mapo tofu**, though I have no photo of it. With it I had **a black tea beer**, two bottles. I went in meaning to eat lightly, and once again I left full.

## The black tea beer, Hao'sum Beer

On the label, inside an oval border, are the words "HAO'SUM BEER", the Chinese characters 好心食館 and, below them, "BLACK TEA". It is an IPA, the ingredients are printed as "BLACK TEA · TANGERINE · BEER", and the Korean text reads 호우섬 비어, 5.3% ALC, 330ml. The side of one bottle also carries the words "HAO'SUM × GORILLA". I ordered it as a drink to have between the dumplings and the stir-fry, and it was better than I expected.

## Menu words

- **Dim sum (點心)** are bite-sized Cantonese dishes served in bamboo steamers
- **Soup dumplings (小籠包, xiao long bao)** are steamed dumplings with meat and broth sealed inside a thin wrapper. The broth runs out when you bite, so it is safer to eat them from a spoon
- **Har gow (蝦餃)** is a Cantonese dim sum made with shrimp. The one I ordered had a black wrapper, listed as "black"
- **Water spinach (空心菜)** is a vegetable named for its hollow stems, and Chinese restaurants often stir-fry it with garlic
- **Clay-pot rice (煲仔飯, bou zai faan)** is a Hong Kong-style rice dish cooked in a small pot
- **Hao'sum (好心, Cantonese hou sam)** is a phrase meaning "a kind heart" or "good will"

## Before you go

- **Location**: B1, Times Square, 15 Yeongjung-ro, Yeongdeungpo-gu, Seoul. Times Square is linked to Yeongdeungpo Station (Line 1) by an underground passage, so it is an easy walk
- **Hours**: 11:00 – 21:30, phone 0507-1440-7801 (as of Oct 2026)
- **Prices**: soup dumplings 7,500 won, black har gow (3 pieces) 9,500 won, the pork and mapo tofu clay-pot rice 17,000 won and a bottle of Hao'sum Beer 7,500 won. Leaving out the water spinach, what I ordered that day (with two beers) came to 49,000 won. The crispy râpée chicken on the menu card in my photo is 27,900 won (as of Oct 2026)
- **Quantity**: even if you plan a light meal, adding a stir-fry and a clay-pot rice to the dim sum quickly makes a full spread

## For foreign visitors

- The menu is written in Korean and English side by side, with prices in won
- Dim sum comes in different piece counts per basket (the har gow comes as 3), so it suits ordering several kinds to share
- When you order, just saying the dish name works: **"Sorongpo juseyo (소롱포 주세요)"**
- The bottled beer (Hao'sum Beer) is 5.3% alcohol, 330ml
