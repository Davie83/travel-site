**A large bakery cafe tucked into the Ui-dong valley, at the foot of Bukhansan.** It is a brick building with a garden, and after dark the garden is strung with lights.

## Among hikers and the weekend crowd

It sits inside the Ui-dong valley. The area has plenty of places to eat, so it **gets busy on weekends**, and a lot of hikers pass through on their way up and down the mountain. High Ground runs its bakery cafe right in the middle of that.

It is **quite large, and the interior is well done.** Because it is popular, **the car park is big as well.**

## A bite of bread, a cup of tea

**There is a real range of breads.** Sit down with a cup of tea and bread from a master baker, and you get a short, easy break.

![Two iced coffees and bread from High Ground Bakery — a long roll with butter cream, a cream-topped dessert and a spiral pastry on one tray, with a paper bag printed HIGH QUALITY HIGH GROUND beside it](/assets/img/seoul-highground-bakery-coffee.jpg)

On a price-listing site, the Bukhansan pinecone bread (북한산솔방울빵) and the red-yeast-rice cream bun (홍국슈크림빵) are each listed at **5,900 won** (checked as of Oct 2026), and Google Maps shows **10,000–20,000 won per person**. With so many breads on display, it is easier to look and choose at the counter.

## Getting there

You can walk from **Bukhansanui Station** (Ui-Sinseol Line). On the map it is around a kilometre, uphill toward the valley, so **if you have time, it works as a pleasant walk.**

By car, the car park is large and many reviews say it is free. To avoid wrong turns, search **"하이그라운드 제빵소"** in your map app.

## It works after dark too

The official Instagram lists it as open **every day from 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM**, all year round (as of Oct 2026).

The main photo was taken at night: lights strung over the garden, and the building lit up with a **HIGH GROUND** sign and a sign promising freshly baked bread. It feels different from the valley by day, and suits a stop after a hike or at the end of an evening walk.

## For visitors from abroad

- **A bakery cafe** is a large cafe that sells its own baked bread alongside coffee. In Korea you usually pick bread from the display and order and pay at the counter
- **Myeongjang (名匠, "master craftsman")** is an official title Korea gives to highly skilled tradespeople. This place is known for bread from a master baker
- The sign reading **HIGH GROUND** is this place. In Korean map apps it comes up as **"하이그라운드 제빵소"**
- It is on the **Bukhansan side of the city**, not in central Seoul, so allow extra travel time
