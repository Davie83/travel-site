## What you're actually buying

You order one crab. **What arrives is a full table.** That is how this place works, and it changes how you should think about the price.

Order snow crab or king crab and the course runs:

- **Juk** — rice porridge, to start
- **Salad**
- **Mushroom sweet-and-sour** (a vegetarian take on *tangsuyuk*)
- **Corn cheese** — sweet corn baked under melted cheese. A Korean bar-food staple
- **Sashimi**
- **Fried prawns**
- **Grilled abalone**
- Then **the crab**

![The side dishes that come with the crab — sashimi, fried prawn, seasoned fish](/assets/img/seoul-kimnkim-daege-table.jpg)

The table keeps filling while the crab is being steamed. **You are not paying for a crab and nothing else** — that's the point worth understanding before you look at the price.

## How much crab to order

Hard to judge from a menu, so here's the benchmark.

**For two people, 1.5–2kg of snow crab is plenty.** Because so much else arrives, you don't need to scale the crab up. That amount leaves two people full and having eaten a lot of different things.

## On price — there's a promotion right now

**As of 2026 the restaurant is running a 20th-anniversary promotion.**

- **Snow crab — ₩79,000 per kg**
- **King crab — ₩119,000 per kg**

The owner's notice on Google says they are offering the crab course **"at ten-year-ago prices"** and have cut their margin to do it.

> **Check before you go that it's still running.** Promotions end.

Outside the promotion, Google's registered bracket is **₩100,000+ per person.** Crab is an expensive ingredient anywhere; this was never a cheap restaurant.

But **the table as served feels like being looked after.** So, plainly: this is not a place to eat cheaply. It's a place to spend money and be treated well.

## Private rooms

**There are several private rooms**, which is the practical reason to pick this place for an occasion.

A reviewer put it well: good enough for family, for hosting someone, or for a couple. **Being able to avoid a noisy table in the middle of Hongdae** is genuinely useful — this is one of Seoul's loudest nightlife districts.

## Finding it, and other practical notes

**Know the building before you arrive.** The ground floor holds the live tanks. **Dining is on the 3rd floor** — take the lift. People do walk in, see only tanks, and assume they're in the wrong place.

- **Hongik Univ. Station** is close. The Airport Railroad stops there, so you can come straight from Incheon Airport
- **Parking is tight** — reviewers mention this repeatedly. Take the subway
- **Reservations** go through Naver. Book ahead if you want a room
- **Closes at 10:00 PM**
- **Not spicy** — the crab and most of the course carry no chilli heat

## Who this suits

- ✅ You want crab served as a full course, not just a boiled crab
- ✅ You need a quiet private room for an occasion
- ✅ You're hosting someone and want the table to look after them
- ✅ You can't eat spicy food
- ⚠️ Not a cheap meal — even at promotion prices, budget ₩50,000–80,000 per person
- ⚠️ Parking is difficult, and dining is up on the 3rd floor

> Based on a personal visit. The promotion is time-limited and prices and hours change often, so please confirm before you go.
