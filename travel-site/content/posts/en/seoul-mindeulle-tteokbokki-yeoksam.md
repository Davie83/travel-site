I used to really dislike rice cakes. Somehow my taste has changed, though, and these days, when it comes to a snack, I would pick rice cakes over bread. I honestly do not know why. On one of those days, I went to Mindeulle Tteokbokki near Yeoksam Station for lunch.

## 🏢 A long-running place, full of office workers at lunch

**It has been around for a while, so I suspect most people who live or work nearby have been here at least once.** At lunchtime, most of the customers were office workers.

## 🍢 A safe order: fish cake tteokbokki and assorted tempura

I looked over the menu and went with a safe choice: odeng tteokbokki and assorted tempura. The tteokbokki is the cook-at-the-table kind, with the pot set on a burner on your table.

![Four skewered fish cake sheets laid across a pot of cook-at-the-table tteokbokki on a burner, with ramen noodles and a boiled egg underneath and shredded vegetables in a yellow pot on the left](/assets/img/seoul-mindeulle-tteokbokki-yeoksam-skewers.jpg)

When it first arrives, four wide sheets of fish cake on skewers lie side by side across the pot, with ramen noodles and a boiled egg peeking out below.

![Tongs stirring cabbage in the tteokbokki pot, with tempura in a wire basket on the right](/assets/img/seoul-mindeulle-tteokbokki-yeoksam-cabbage.jpg)

Once it starts to boil, you stir the heap of cabbage into the sauce with tongs. After the skewered fish cake is cut into bite-size pieces and goes back in, the pot is packed with square pieces of fish cake by the time it is done.

## 🍤 Moderately spicy, with clean-tasting tempura

**The tteokbokki was moderately spicy, and the tempura tasted clean.** The tempura comes in a paper-lined wire basket.

## 🍳 Most tables finished with egg fried rice; I skipped it

Looking around, many tables finished with egg fried rice. I did not think my stomach could handle it, so I did not order any.

## 🌐 For visitors

- Odeng tteokbokki is tteokbokki with plenty of fish cake (odeng), cooked yourself on the burner at your table. To order, say "오뎅떡볶이 주세요 (Odeng tteokbokki juseyo)"
- Tongs and scissors are on the table, so you can stir the pot as it boils and cut the fish cake yourself
- The nearest station is Yeoksam Station (Line 2, 역삼역). At lunchtime, many customers are office workers from nearby

## 🚶 If you are passing by

"Will I think of it again next time I am in the area?" I wondered, after another good lunch. If you want a cook-at-the-table tteokbokki place that you finish with fried rice, there is also [Sunny Tteokbokki — Fried Rice Fills the Gap](/en/posts/seoul-sunny-jeuktteokbokki-yeouido) in Yeouido.
