I like animals. I haven't kept a pet since I was a child, but I ended up stopping by this place.

The fact that it's the **"Hongdae branch"** suggests there are others.

## A break with the cats

At a cat cafe you order a drink and stay in the space with the cats. The cats here were used to being around people.

![Roofcatme Hongdae — three cats resting on the herringbone tile floor](/assets/img/seoul-roofcatme-hongdae-2.jpg)

**If you like cats**, it's a good place to sit and rest for a bit while you're going around Hongdae.

## Location

It's **close to Hongik Univ. Station Exit 9**. There were **plenty of foreign visitors** when I went.

## For foreign visitors

- At a **cat cafe**, the usual model is that you pay admission (or a mandatory one drink) and stay for a set amount of time. Check the exact fee and limit on arrival.
- The basic etiquette is **not to force-hold or wake the cats**. Let them sniff your hand before you touch.
- Many places have you sanitise your hands on the way in. Follow the staff's instructions.
- A short walk from **Hongik Univ. Station Exit 9**. It's on a basement floor.

## Who this suits

- ✅ You like cats and have no chance to be around one while travelling
- ✅ You want **an indoor break** while walking Hongdae
- ✅ You need somewhere to pass time in Hongdae on a rainy day
- ⚠️ You have animal allergies
- ⚠️ You expect lively, playful cats — in the daytime most may be asleep

## Before you go

- **Location** — near Hongik Univ. Station Exit 9, basement level.
- **Admission · time limit · closing day** — I couldn't confirm them this time. Check the map or call before you go.
- **Etiquette** — don't wake or pick up the cats. Treats only per the cafe's rules.

> Based on a personal visit. Admission, time limits, closing days and branch details can change — check before you go.
