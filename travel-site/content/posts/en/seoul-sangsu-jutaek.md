**This is a good place for an easy drink.** Calling it a Korean-style izakaya is probably the closest fit.

## A second-floor bar in a converted old house

It's on the second floor of an old house down a side street near Sangsu Station. The sign is small enough that it's easy to walk past the first time — the stairs are right next to a clothing shop on the ground floor. There's also a terrace, good for sitting outside on a nice day.

![The entrance to Sangsu Jutaek — stairs up to the second floor of an old house, with a sandwich board out front listing the menu](/assets/img/seoul-sangsu-jutaek-storefront.jpg)

## The makgeolli lineup is wide

**This is especially good news if you like makgeolli.** The lineup is wide enough to compare several side by side, so it's a good place to try makgeolli you haven't had before. If you're a foreign visitor, I'd especially recommend it for trying a range of Korean makgeolli in one sitting.

## The anju menu spans a lot of ground

The anju menu is wide, so there's fun in picking a few different things. I ordered the **Sangsu octopus bossam** — octopus and boiled pork wrapped with perilla leaf — and the **seafood minari-jeon**, loaded with minari and vegetables under a sweet-spicy sauce. Beyond that there's also clam jjim, cheese kimchi-jeon and beef tendon fish cake soup, so soups, pancakes and steamed dishes are all covered.

## Solidly good, not spectacular

The anju itself isn't the kind of thing you'd brag about elsewhere. But it isn't overly salty or heavy-handed either — solidly good enough to go with makgeolli or other traditional liquor. Think of this less as a place that wins on one standout dish, and more as somewhere to enjoy the drink and the atmosphere together.

## Before you go

- **The makgeolli lineup is wide** — good for comparing a few side by side
- **The anju menu spans a lot of ground** — bossam, jeon, jjim and soup are all on offer
- **It's on the 2nd floor, easy to miss the sign** — take the stairs next to the ground-floor clothing shop
- **About a 4-minute walk from Sangsu Station Exit 1**
- Nearby public parking is paid, so public transport is easier
