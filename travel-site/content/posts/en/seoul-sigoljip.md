At Sigoljip in Euljiro's rib alley, the staff grill the LA galbi for you, and when it is done they snip it with scissors and hand it over.

## 🔥 The staff do the grilling, and that is the strength

What this place does best is grill LA galbi just right and make it taste good. Even after a long gap, the doneness is handled with skill. The meat gets a browned, seared surface and stays tender inside, and they cut it with scissors at exactly the right moment. They know when to flip it before the marinade burns and turns bitter. You don't need to know how to grill, and you don't have to bother with it.

## 🍖 The table over the grill

LA galbi is beef short rib cut thinly across the bone and marinated, one kind of Korean grilled galbi. In the photo, bone-in LA galbi is piled up on a black cast-iron grill. The surface is a deep brown, nicely browned with a few charred spots, and the white cross-section of the bone shows. A portable gas canister stands behind the grill, and plates of onion, a pot of stew, seasoned bean sprouts and pickles are arranged around it.

![The LA galbi pan with doenjang stew and side dishes](/assets/img/seoul-sigoljip-table.jpg)

## 🍶 After round one, round two

I had suyuk (boiled pork slices) and drinks at the old sundae-guk spot [Sansu Gapsan](/en/posts/seoul-sansu-gapsan) in Euljiro (round one), then walked over to this place. It was just after the evening rush, so I got in without a wait. Even after a long gap the seating is comfortable, and the worn, old-alley mood of Euljiro 3-ga is exactly as I remembered. Already full, I skipped the famous omelette and ordered only the LA galbi, with soju alongside.

## 👥 Three or four people is just right

Three or four people felt just right. For a big group, I think it would be crowded and loud. Galbi is usually ordered from two servings up.

## 🚇 The downside is the distance

These days it is in a neighbourhood I don't get to often, so I only stop by when I have something to do nearby. That is my one complaint. It is a short walk from Euljiro 3-ga Station (Lines 2 and 3), and within ten minutes of Myeongdong.

## 🌐 For visitors

- LA galbi is beef short rib sawn thinly across the bone in the American-style LA cut and marinated in soy sauce, sugar and garlic. It is **not spicy**; it is salty with a little sweetness
- The staff grill it, so you don't need to know how. When it is done they snip it up with scissors, and you eat it with lettuce or side dishes
- Meat on the bone is fine to pick up and eat with your hands
- The famous side here is **gyeranmari** (a rolled omelette). If it is your first time, it is worth adding one

## 🧭 Who it's for

- ✅ People who like soy-marinated grilled galbi — good if you want Korean barbecue that isn't spicy
- ✅ People who find grilling meat a bother — the staff do it for you
- ✅ People who want a plate of meat as a second stop after drinks in the Euljiro alleys
- ✅ People with foreign friends — it needs almost no explaining
- ⚠️ People trying to eat cheaply — galbi is priced per serving, and rice, cold noodles or the omelette push the bill up more than you'd think
- ⚠️ People who want something fiery — this is a salty-sweet soy marinade
