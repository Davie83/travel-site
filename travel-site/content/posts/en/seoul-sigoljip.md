An old place in Euljiro's galbi alley. After drinks nearby, I came by as a second stop for the first time in a while.

## After Sansu Gapsan, a second stop

We had our first round — boiled offal and a drink — at **Sansu Gapsan**, an old sundae-guk (blood sausage soup) place in Euljiro, then walked over here. Coming back after a long gap, the seats are still comfortable and the worn-alley feel of Euljiro 3-ga is unchanged.

Already full, I skipped the **gyeranmari** this place is known for and ordered only the **LA galbi**.

## Just the LA galbi — leave the grilling to them

LA galbi is beef short rib sliced thin across the bone (the "LA cut") and steeped in a soy marinade. It's grilled on a domed pan like the one in the photo.

Here, **the staff grill it for you.** Even after a long time away, they judge it well — a proper browned crust outside, not chewy inside, cut with scissors at just the right moment. They know when to turn it before the marinade scorches bitter.

![The LA galbi pan with doenjang stew and side dishes](/assets/img/seoul-sigoljip-table.jpg)

The sides come with a pot of doenjang stew and an assortment of namul and kimchi. The seasoning isn't strong, so it's easy going even if you can't take spice.

## For visitors

- **LA galbi** is beef short rib in the American "LA cut" — sliced thin across the bone — steeped in a soy, sugar and garlic marinade. **Not spicy**; salty with a slight sweetness.
- The staff grill it, so you don't need to know how. When it's done they cut it with scissors; eat it with the lettuce and side dishes.
- The bone is still attached, so it's fine to pick it up by hand. Working the meat off the bone is the normal way to eat it.
- This place is known for its side of **gyeranmari** (rolled omelette). I skipped it this time, full, but it's worth adding on a first visit.
- It's in the **Euljiro 3-ga** rib alley — a short walk from Euljiro 3-ga Station (subway lines 2 and 3), and under ten minutes from Myeongdong.

## Who it's for

- ✅ Anyone who likes **soy-marinated grilled galbi** — a good fit if you want Korean BBQ that isn't spicy
- ✅ Anyone who finds grilling meat a chore — the staff handle it
- ✅ Anyone wanting a plate of meat as a **second stop** on an Euljiro drinking night
- ✅ Anyone with non-Korean company — the dish needs almost no explaining
- ⚠️ Anyone after a cheap meal — galbi is priced per portion, and rice, naengmyeon and gyeranmari add up faster than you'd think
- ⚠️ Anyone wanting punchy, spicy flavour — this is sweet-salty soy marinade

## Before you go

- **Ordering** — galbi is usually from two portions. On a first visit, adding a gyeranmari rounds out the table.
- **Price** — I couldn't confirm it this time. Just know that coming in for "a quick drink" and then ordering around can run up a fair bill.
- **Hours · closing day** — not confirmed. Check the map or call before you go.
- **Seating** — it's an old alley place, so it can get crowded in the evening.
