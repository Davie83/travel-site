This is Sunny Tteokbokki in Yeouido. I am honestly not a big fan of flour-based food, but now and then a craving for cook-at-the-table tteokbokki like this hits me.

## 🍲 A little of everything, all just right

The tteokbokki here feels like a bit of everything in just the right amount. It is sweet and spicy enough to hit the craving. The portion is, unfortunately, just about right too, so big eaters who order one per person may find it a little short.

The tteokbokki is cooked in its black pot on a portable burner right at the table. A heap of shredded green leafy vegetables sits on the red broth, with curly ramen noodles showing between them, and two glasses of Cass draft beer stand next to the pot.

## 🍚 Fill the gap with fried rice

The fried rice is good here. So even if it may fall a little short, order one per person and fill the gap with the fried rice, and it is just right.

## 🍺 Draft beer for 3,000 won in Yeouido

When I visited recently, there was a deal of 500 cc of draft beer for 3,000 won (as of Nov 2026). It is a promotion, so I do not know how long it lasts. Draft beer for 3,000 won in Yeouido was a surprise.

## 🌐 For visitors

- Jeukseok tteokbokki (즉석떡볶이) is tteokbokki cooked at the table in a pot over a burner. To order, say "즉석떡볶이 주세요 (Jeukseok tteokbokki juseyo)"
- The nearest station is National Assembly Station (Line 9, 국회의사당역), and you can also get there from Yeouido Station (Lines 5 and 9, 여의도역)
- It is sweet and spicy, and I felt it is a taste foreign visitors can eat too, though spice tolerance differs by person

## 👥 Who it suits

It is not "insanely delicious", but it is a place I strangely think of from time to time. It suits anyone who wants to order one per person and finish with fried rice, and anyone who wants cheap draft beer and tteokbokki in Yeouido. For another pot dish in Yeouido, there is [Jungang Haejang Yeouido — Premium Beef Tripe Hot Pot](/en/posts/seoul-jungang-haejang-yeouido).
