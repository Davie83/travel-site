**This is a taco spot right outside Yeouido Station.** Easy to grab something quick and filling here.

## It's a franchise, so there are several branches

The Taco Booth is a franchise with branches beyond Yeouido. It's even been featured on the TV show "Life's Masters" (생활의 달인) as one of Seoul's four great taco spots — worth keeping in mind that the vibe and the wait can vary branch to branch.

![The sign at The Taco Booth Yeouido Station — a neon logo mounted on tile, with "TASTY NEW YORK" below it](/assets/img/seoul-taco-booth-yeouido-detail.jpg)

## Tacos, poke bowls, burritos — and sets for 2 or 3

The menu runs on **tacos**, **poke bowls**, and **burritos**. Good for a light solo meal, but there are also set menus built for two or three people to share.

## Fresh ingredients, and a lunchtime beer makes it better

The ingredients are fresh and the food is solid here. Pair it with a beer at lunch and it's about as good as it gets.

## Tacos over a burger when you've got no appetite

Personally, on a day I have no appetite, I'd reach for tacos over a burger. There's a generous pile of vegetables on top, so it eats light instead of sitting heavy.

## Right outside Yeouido Station Exit 6

It's on the first floor of the Eyelex building, visible as soon as you step out of Yeouido Station's Exit 6. There's outdoor seating too, good for a nice-weather evening, and there's usually a wait at both lunch and dinner. Parking in the Eyelex building's rear 1F lot gets you 30 minutes free at lunch, 1 hour free at dinner.

## For foreign visitors

- A **taco** is a tortilla topped with meat, vegetables, and salsa; a **quesadilla** is a tortilla grilled with cheese and fillings folded inside; a **burrito** is a tortilla wrapped around the fillings
- There's a mildly spicy kick from the salsa and sauces, but overall it's not very hot (1 out of 5)
- Forks are provided, so use a fork and knife if eating with your hands isn't comfortable

## Who this is for

- ✅ Anyone who wants something quick and filling in a short window
- ✅ Anyone needing a light meal on a low-appetite day
- ✅ Anyone who wants a beer alongside lunch
- ✅ Anyone going with a group who wants to share a variety off a set menu
- ⚠️ Anyone who can't handle any spice at all — there's a mild kick from the salsa and sauces
- ⚠️ Anyone wanting a quiet meal — there's usually a wait at both lunch and dinner

> This is a first-hand account, and taste is subjective. Prices and hours are **(as of Oct 2026)** — worth checking again before you go.
