**I stopped in on a late-winter morning, when spring was not far off.**

## The room catches you first

When I think of this place, **the space comes to mind before the coffee does.**

The photo is that impression. Cups lined up on dark wood shelving, and along the bar a grinder, drippers, and glass jars of beans in a row. You can see at a glance that someone has fussed over this room.

## One americano

**I ordered an americano in that cool weather, and the flavour of the coffee and the atmosphere of the place both stayed with me.**

One thing I should declare, though. **I like coffee with acidity.**

Coffee taste splits people, and acidity splits them most sharply of all — some seek it out, some avoid it. It seems more useful to tell you where my impression is coming from than to pretend it is neutral.

## Being honest about the price

**Coffee here is not cheap.** If you arrive with neighbourhood-cafe prices in mind, it will feel expensive.

Even so, **if you are nearby, it is worth stopping in for one cup.** This is less a place to travel across the city for, and more **a good place to know about when you are already in that area.**

## Seating and location

**There are not many tables.** Some reviews call that a plus — it never feels crowded — but it also means you may not get a seat.

The location is good. The **Bank of Korea Money Museum** is right there, and Shinsegae main store, Namdaemun Market and Myeongdong are all within walking distance. One review says the view out of the window feels European.

It works well as **somewhere to sit for a while** in the middle of a Myeongdong walk.

## For international visitors

- **An americano is espresso with hot water added.** It is the most commonly ordered coffee in Korea
- This shop works with **beans that carry acidity.** If you want a dark, bitter-forward cup, ask before ordering
- **Takeaway is available.** If there is no seat, take it with you
- **There are few tables.** For a larger group, somewhere else may suit better
- **No phone number is registered**, so checking ahead is difficult
- **The closing day could not be confirmed.** Keep that in mind if you are travelling far
- In most Korean cafes you **order and pay at the counter first**. Sitting down and waiting will not get you served

## Go if

- ✅ You like **coffee with acidity**
- ✅ You enjoy a room as much as a cup
- ✅ You have a **Myeongdong, Namdaemun or Bank of Korea** stop planned
- ✅ You want somewhere quiet, alone or as a pair
