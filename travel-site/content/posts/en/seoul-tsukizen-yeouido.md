I have been about five times, mostly at weekday lunch, and there has always been a considerable wait at lunch. This is Tsukizen Yeouido, a tonkatsu specialist.

## 🕛 Weekday lunch has a wait, and going alone is hard

Weekday lunch is crowded, so going alone is hard. If you want to go alone, it's better to go on a weekday outside meal times. To be safe, get there before 11:10.

The Yeouido branch sits in a corner inside **B1 of TP Tower**. It connects directly to Yeouido Station underground, so it's easy to find, and if you give your licence plate at the TP Tower counter you get one hour of free parking. When I went on a weekend it was quiet, which was nice, but the current listing shows the Yeouido branch closed on weekends (as of Oct 2026), so check ahead if you plan a weekend visit.

## 🧆 The menchikatsu is softer than elsewhere

The reason this place comes to mind whenever I crave katsu is the menchikatsu. It's softer and has a better meaty flavour than the menchikatsu at other katsu places. The thickness of the meat and the demi-glace sauce combine so well it makes you smile.

![Menchikatsu and roskatsu at Tsukizen Yeouido — menchikatsu draped in demi-glace sauce next to a thick-cut roskatsu, sliced medium and served side by side](/assets/img/seoul-tsukizen-yeouido-detail.jpg)

In the photo, three breaded cutlets sit on a round plate, covered with a thick, glossy, dark brown sauce. The sauce covers the cutlets almost completely, so the breading shows only at the edges. At the top of the plate is finely shredded cabbage with sesame dressing, along with a corn salad and a slice of tomato.

## 🥩 The roskatsu and chateaubriand aren't bad either

The roskatsu and chateaubriand on the menu taste fine too. But thick-cut Japanese-style tonkatsu shops like this have sprung up everywhere lately, so although the taste is clearly good, it didn't move me much. The main branch is said to be between Hapjeong and Sangsu, but I haven't been.

## 🥗 A Yeouido meal route

It's within walking distance of Yeouido Station, so it combines well with other meals the same day. On days when katsu feels heavy, [Konthai Yeouido](/en/posts/seoul-konthai-yeouido) (Thai food in the IFC Mall) or [Hadongkwan](/en/posts/seoul-hadongkwan) (clear gomtang) are lighter options, and if you want tacos instead of burgers there is [The Taco Booth Yeouido](/en/posts/seoul-taco-booth-yeouido). All are in the same Yeouido area.

## 🌐 For visitors

- Menchikatsu is ground beef formed and deep-fried, then covered in rich demi-glace sauce, a Western-style Japanese katsu. It differs in character from ordinary Japanese tonkatsu
- Roskatsu and chateaubriand are classic Japanese-style tonkatsu, fried thick and sliced against the grain. You eat them with wasabi, salt and sauce
- It isn't spicy at all
- To avoid the weekday lunch line, go early. The current listing has it closed on weekends, so look elsewhere for a weekend plan

It suits people curious about menchikatsu in demi-glace and anyone needing a proven katsu meal in Yeouido. If you have already tried several Japanese-style katsu spots, the taste is good but may not be a special thrill.
