**A Western restaurant near Jeongdong-gil.** The atmosphere is charming enough that it's worth a stop just to sit, or to take photos.

## A restaurant with its own garden — the setting is different from the start

The old brick building has plants and décor tucked into every corner, so the place is a pleasure to look at from the moment you walk in. The decorations seem to change with the season — when I went, it was in full Christmas mode. There's also a separate lunch set, good for a lighter visit.

![The entrance to Urban Garden — an old brick building decked out with Christmas décor and potted plants](/assets/img/seoul-urban-garden-storefront.jpg)

## I ordered steak and pasta

This time, instead of the lunch set, I ordered **steak and pasta**. The steak came with mushrooms, potatoes and mustard on the side; the pasta was a cream sauce topped with scallops.

## The setting comes before the taste

Honestly, the food itself isn't the kind of thing you'd rave about elsewhere. But **the atmosphere is good enough that almost anything would taste good here**, so overall it was still an enjoyable meal. If you're after a nice setting more than a must-eat dish, this will land better for you.

## A walk along Jeongdong-gil and Gwanghwamun afterward

**Jeongdong-gil** runs right outside, good for a walk after the meal, and a short walk further gets you to **Gwanghwamun**. A good route to pair with a date or a casual meetup.

## Before you go

- **The setting is the strength here** — recommended if you like taking photos
- **There's a lunch set too** — check the set menu if you want a lighter visit
- **Pairs well with a walk along Jeongdong-gil and on to Gwanghwamun** afterward
- **About a 4-minute walk from Seodaemun Station (Line 5)**
- Prices and hours can shift by season — check before you go
