It's a place I stop at, almost every time, even briefly, whenever I'm in Gangwon. This time too, I pulled over on the way past.

## ㅅㅊ, and the ferris wheel

A large **ㅅㅊ** sculpture stands in front of the sand. That alone will tell a lot of people where this is (ㅅ and ㅊ are the first consonants of "Sokcho" in Korean). If you're still not sure — one more hint: once you see the **ferris wheel** beside the beach, there's no mistaking it. This is Sokcho Beach.

Next to the sculpture there's a frame-shaped photo spot that catches **Jodo** islet inside it, and a **#SokchoTrip** sign. People take turns standing there for photos.

## A place I stop at, even briefly, on a Gangwon trip

It's not somewhere you come to do anything in particular. You walk a loop of the plaza, put your feet in the sand for a bit, look at the sea, and leave. And still — **one visit here lifts the mood.** It's the kind of spot that makes it feel like you're actually on a trip.

The surroundings always greet you the same way: the paved stone plaza, the palm trees, the breakwater, the sea in front of it. Nothing much changes, and that sameness is itself part of why it's easy to be here.

## The Sokcho Eye and around the beach

![The Sokcho Eye ferris wheel beside the beach, with a building of cafes and a bakery — Sokcho Beach](/assets/img/sokcho-beach-ferriswheel.jpg)

Right beside the beach is the **Sokcho Eye** ferris wheel. By day, the white frame with its coloured capsules turning photographs well; in the evening it lights up.

The lower floors of the building it stands on hold **coffee, bakery, beer and ice cream** shops — you'll see names like "Sokcho Sand" and "Cafe Whale Light" on the signs. It's a good spot to duck into for a coffee while walking the beach.

The beach itself is only open for swimming during the summer season; the rest of the year you're mostly here for the plaza, the promenade, and the photo spots.

## For visitors

- **Sokcho** is a city on the east coast, reachable from Seoul by express bus or by KTX plus a local bus. Sokcho Beach is close to the city centre.
- The **ㅅㅊ (Korean consonants) sculpture** and the **Sokcho Eye** ferris wheel are this beach's main photo spots.
- **Swimming is only possible in the summer season.** Other times of year it's walking, photos and cafes.
- The building right next to it has cafes, a bakery and a beer shop, so it's a fine place to pass time even in poor weather.
- Nearby are Sokcho Tourist & Fishery Market, Yeonggeumjeong, and Abai Village — easy to string into a half-day.

## Who it's for

- ✅ Anyone who wants a **quick look at the sea** on a Sokcho / Gangwon trip
- ✅ Anyone who wants photos at the **photo spots** — the ㅅㅊ sculpture, the Jodo frame, #SokchoTrip
- ✅ Anyone who wants to ride the ferris wheel, or have **a coffee with a sea view**
- ✅ Anyone who wants a **well-kept, easy-to-walk beach plaza**
- ⚠️ Anyone after a quiet, undeveloped stretch of coast — this is a tidied-up tourist spot
- ⚠️ Anyone expecting to swim outside summer — you can't go in the water

## Before you go

- **Location** — Sokcho, Gangwon State. Close to central Sokcho, with several paid car parks nearby.
- **Season** — the beach operates only in summer. Other times, it's the plaza, promenade and ferris wheel.
- **Combine with** — Sokcho Tourist & Fishery Market, Yeonggeumjeong, and Abai Village for a half-day.
- **Hours · fees** — ferris wheel and parking fees and hours vary by season, so check before you go.
